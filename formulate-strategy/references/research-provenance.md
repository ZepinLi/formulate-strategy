# Research Provenance

Updated on 2026-11-07. Read this reference when explaining, auditing, or revising the skill's methodology. The skill operationalizes the sources as testable guidance; it does not treat any author as unquestionable authority. Ordinary strategy work loads only the relevant operational references, not this entire source collection.

## Source architecture

Paul Graham is the primary lens because his essays connect the choice of work with curiosity, aptitude, self-directed projects, creation, exploration, independent judgment, returns, user contact, and founder survival. Sam Altman's interview with Raj Shamani contributes practitioner examples of calibration, commitment, progression, and success costs. Independent work on strategic focus, purpose and allocation, affordable loss, staged validation, base rates, and the outside view bounds both lenses.

The synthesis is:

> Find worthwhile questions, explore through bounded real work, calibrate judgment, concentrate on one current direction, develop through owned work and a credible return mechanism, and protect both the survival floor and the life the user values.

This is an operational synthesis, not a quotation from any source.

## Evidence hierarchy for a person's direction

Prefer, in order:

1. Repeated behavior and completed work; relevant research scrutiny, user or beneficiary behavior, retention, payment, or verified economics according to the work
2. Direct observation, interviews tied to actual behavior, and small discriminating experiments
3. Demonstrated learning rate, credible reference classes, and comparable-case evidence
4. First-party essays, practitioner frameworks, and well-documented experience
5. Introspection without action, credentials, popularity, analogy, slogans, and fashionable narratives

Interests and intuitions generate hypotheses. Work and external response test them. A base rate constrains confidence but does not replace case-specific causal evidence.

Match the burden of proof to the commitment. A bounded experiment can be justified by a worthwhile uncertainty; concentrated resources need stronger relevant support. Neither commercial demand nor proof of a perpetual growth loop is a universal prerequisite for valuable research or creative work.

## Applicability limits of the Graham lens

- The essays draw heavily from Y Combinator, software startups, and highly ambitious creative work. Survivorship and selection effects limit generalization.
- Interest is not destiny. It must survive sustained work, difficulty, opportunity cost, and feedback from people affected by the work.
- Superlinear outcomes are uneven and uncertain. They do not justify exposing health, relationships, legal duties, or financial survival to unbounded loss.
- Startup growth is not sufficient evidence by itself. Retention, user value, acquisition quality, economics, and runway constrain the thesis.
- Founder involvement is stage- and context-dependent. Information proximity and accountability are the useful hypotheses; permanent control of every decision is not.
- A startup is only one possible owned project. Research, craft, a creator body of work, open-source software, and internal ventures may also compound agency and impact.

## Method boundaries

- One Current Growth Focus is a discipline for allocating scarce resources now, not a lifelong identity.
- A Provisional Exploration Focus answers one direction-choice question through bounded owned work. It may compare projects across fields, normally sequentially, and has explicit reconsideration and convergence conditions.
- The Growth and Return Mechanism distinguishes compounding, a valuable threshold, and their combination. Compounding requires a causal loop; a threshold requires an explanation of its value, progress, costs, and risks. Neither guarantees exponential growth.
- Integrity gates are proportionate to the decision stage. An affordable exploration names what it will test rather than pretending to have demonstrated fit, demand, or a mature return mechanism already.
- Success costs join downside risk: valuable creation must remain compatible with the user's own life priorities.
- Personal growth is not generic self-help. The skill must connect direction to valuable creation, owned work, real feedback, trade-offs, and survival.
- Founder strategy here concerns the founder's growth, reality contact, ownership, and startup survival. It does not replace mature-company, platform, product, or organization strategy.

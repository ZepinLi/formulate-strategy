## Limits

This is one response per request per version, with fresh-conversation instructions inside two agent batches rather than independent model processes for each case. The implementer assessed the answers after generation and knew which version was newer. No repeated stochastic trials, multi-turn user outcomes, or automatic discovery benchmark were run. The full-report format was inspected in the instructions, not exercised as a separately requested report.

Several new requests contain clear cues, and the reference intentionally includes related research and founder examples. Good performance here does not prove generalization or superiority. The six-step revision is justified as the requested clarification and reorganization; retain these artifacts for review and re-run affected cases when actual usage reveals a failure or a material instruction change warrants it.
